Frequently asked questions

How do I report a bug or issue in an app?

Email us at contact@aurict.com with the app name, a description of the issue, and your device model and OS version if possible. Screenshots or screen recordings help us resolve issues faster.

How do I delete my account or data?

Visit our account deletion page and select your app to start the process. If your app isn't listed, email us directly and we'll take care of it.

Where can I read the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service?

Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are available at any time.

How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond within 1-2 business days. For urgent account or billing issues, please include as much detail as possible in your first message.
